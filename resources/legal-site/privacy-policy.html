Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Important: This policy describes VIRA's intended data practices. Keep the live product, permissions, vendors and retention settings consistent with this policy.

1. Who we are

VIRA is an AI-assisted video creation, content planning and social publishing service operated by Synteric ("VIRA", "we", "us" or "our"). Privacy enquiries: privacy@synteric.co.uk.

2. Information we may collect

  • Account data: name, email, profile details, account identifiers and authentication information.
  • Connected-platform data: account identifiers, profile information made available by authorised APIs, OAuth tokens, granted scopes and connection status for services such as TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or Facebook.
  • User content: prompts, scripts, captions, images, audio, video, brand information, publishing instructions and uploaded files.
  • Publishing data: schedules, destinations, post identifiers, status, captions, hashtags and performance data where permitted.
  • Technical data: IP address, device/browser information, timestamps, logs, crash data and security events.
  • Billing data: subscription status and transaction references. Card data is handled by payment processors where used.

3. How we use information

  • provide, secure and maintain VIRA;
  • generate or transform content at your request;
  • connect authorised social accounts and perform requested actions;
  • schedule, publish and track content where enabled;
  • provide support and prevent fraud or abuse;
  • measure product performance and improve user-facing features;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

4. Legal bases

Where UK GDPR or similar laws apply, we generally process data because it is necessary to provide the service, because we have a legitimate interest in operating and securing VIRA, because you consented, or because processing is required by law.

5. Third parties

VIRA may interact with third-party platforms only after you authorise the connection. We may also use providers for cloud hosting, storage, AI generation, media processing, analytics, communications, security and payments. We limit disclosures to what is reasonably necessary.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use data obtained from connected social-platform APIs to create advertising profiles or train general-purpose AI models. User-provided content may be processed by AI or media providers only as needed to provide requested features and subject to their applicable data terms.

6. Google/YouTube data

If VIRA accesses Google or YouTube user data, our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the applicable Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

7. TikTok and other integrations

When you connect TikTok or another supported service, VIRA may receive identifiers, permissions, tokens and content/publishing information allowed by that service. We use it only to provide the connected features you request. You can revoke access through VIRA where available or through the platform's own settings.

8. OAuth credentials

Authorisation tokens are treated as confidential credentials and protected using reasonable technical and organisational safeguards.

9. Retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Credentials are removed or disabled after disconnection or account deletion, subject to limited backup or legal-retention periods.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, request portability, and withdraw consent. See our Data Deletion page or contact privacy@synteric.co.uk.

11. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards.

12. Security

We use reasonable safeguards including encrypted network transport, access controls, credential protection and monitoring. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

13. Children

VIRA is not intended for children under 16.

14. Changes

We may update this policy as VIRA changes and will update the date above.
