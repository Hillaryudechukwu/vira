Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Request deletion

Email privacy@synteric.co.uk with subject VIRA Data Deletion Request. Include the email address or account identifier used with VIRA. We may verify account ownership before completing the request.

What we delete

Subject to legal and security retention requirements, deletion includes account profile information, stored connected-platform credentials, saved content, generated media, publishing schedules and other account-linked personal information.

Disconnect social accounts

You can also revoke VIRA access from TikTok, Google/YouTube, Meta/Facebook/Instagram or another connected platform. Revoking access prevents future authorised API access but does not automatically delete information already stored by VIRA.

Retention exceptions

We may retain limited information where required for fraud prevention, security, accounting, dispute resolution or legal obligations. Backups may persist for a limited technical cycle.
